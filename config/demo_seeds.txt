# Seed people for scripts/find_demo_pairs.py --seeds config/demo_seeds.txt
#
# One person per line: an exact English (or Spanish) Wikidata label, or a QID. Text after '#'
# is a comment. A line [group] starts a group; --cross recent historical pairs only today's
# famous people with historical figures (the most fun demos).
#
# Names are resolved against the people graph. Ambiguous names (several humans with the same
# label) take the best-connected one, and low-degree matches may be namesakes: both are flagged
# by --resolve-only, so pin a QID where it picks the wrong person.
#
# Why these people: recognisable to a general audience (with a strong Brazilian and Colombian
# selection), and chosen where family, teacher/student, team or institutional links are
# plausible. Whether Wikidata actually records any given link is NOT assumed here; the finder
# checks it on the graph. Influencers mostly connect through family and spouses.
